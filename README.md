# Beyond Superintelligence | GIF-10

**The Govind Intelligence Framework — a proposed control architecture for autonomous AI, frontier systems and enterprise governance.**

> Intelligence is capability. Authority is permission. The two must never be confused.

## Research overview

GIF-10 proposes separating model intelligence from operational authority through three planes: intelligence (models propose), authority (independent controls authorize), and assurance (independent monitoring and intervention). Its proposed action contract binds a task to an identity, objective, permitted operations and resources, expiry, budget and approvals.

## Research status and collaboration

GIF-10 is an AI-assisted conceptual framework and proposed validation protocol, not a tested implementation, certification, peer-reviewed publication or demonstrated solution to superintelligence control. Independent technical feedback and author verification are pending. No affiliation with or endorsement by any AI company, research institution or standards body is claimed.
